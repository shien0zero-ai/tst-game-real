ASHGATE / FOREST STUDY - audio additions, 2026-11-16

Forest field recordings:
"Park ambiences" by Thimras, CC0 1.0.
https://opengameart.org/content/park-ambiences
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
park_ambience_birds.wav, park_ambience_wind.wav (Adelaide public park).
Changes: stereo excerpts, rumble/harshness filtering, level adjustment,
2-second wrap crossfades. These are not recordings of this particular forest.

Footsteps:
"Different steps on wood, stone, leaves, gravel and mud" by TinyWorlds, CC0.
https://opengameart.org/content/different-steps-on-wood-stone-leaves-gravel-and-mud
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
gravel.ogg, mud02.ogg, leaves01.ogg, leaves02.ogg from [kdd]DifferentSteps.zip.
The author's page describes edited public-domain pdsounds.org recordings.
Changes: decoded mono 48kHz PCM, filters, fades and level adjustment.

Weapon handling:
"Gun reload sounds" by SpringySpringo, CC0 1.0.
https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
gunreload1.wav: airsoft pistol recording, not an original M1911 recording.
Changes: separated magazine-out, magazine-in, slide-release and dry-click
transients; mono conversion, filtering, fades and level adjustment.

Gunfire:
"Gunshot Sounds", OpenGameArt listing by Tabasco.
https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds
The listing says CC0, but the actual downloaded sounds.zip includes:
Copyright (c) 2009 Vincent Sevedge
Audio and Visual content is covered under the Creative Commons Attribution
3.0 Unported License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
To retain the archive's attribution requirements, this project treats cz.wav
and its adapted pistol_fire_0/1/2.wav as CC BY 3.0, credits Vincent Sevedge /

Changes: three CZ-52 shot transients isolated, converted to mono 48kHz PCM,
filtered, gated tails, normalized. These are stand-in handgun recordings,
not recordings of an M1911 or ballistically authentic .45 ACP reports.
The creators do not endorse this project.

Original project sounds:
gear_rustle.wav is synthesized filtered noise, and case_soft.wav is a short
synthesized metallic tick with decay. They are not field recordings.

Exact download URLs, original SHA256 checksums, processing details and output
hashes are recorded in unreal/AshgateForest/SourceArt/Audio/manifest.json.
Reproduction: Tools/prepare_audio.py --cache <download/extract directory>.
Decoding uses the installed Unreal Engine libsndfile DLL. No third-party
game audio was extracted, and no additional software was installed.

Gunshot weight revision: pistol_fire_0/1/2 retain the same Vincent Sevedge /
Tabasco attribution and CC BY 3.0 treatment. Further changes: 65Hz high-pass,
330Hz body shelf, original synthesized short 140-to-88Hz impact layer, soft
saturation and a 1.3-second tail. Other audio files are unchanged.
