Replacement gunfire: The Free Firearm Sound Library (2014),
Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras, Matthew Nanney. CC0 1.0.
https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
Public mirror: https://github.com/buddingmonkey/FreeFirearmsSFXLibrary
Mirror LICENSE: https://github.com/buddingmonkey/FreeFirearmsSFXLibrary/blob/main/LICENSE
Prepared Master Sheet.csv identifies A_42P.wav as a near-distance stereo recording
of a 1911 .45 handgun, and A_34P.wav as its mid-distance recording.
New active field_shot_0/1/2 use two actual A_42P shot transients and alternate mic
channels, converted from 96kHz/24-bit to 48kHz/16-bit mono. Only DC/ultrasonic
filtering, onset trim, level normalization and short fades are applied.
No generated low-frequency impact, saturation, deliberate bass boost or pitch
shift is added. This replaces the previous CZ-52-based designed gunfire.
The recording is a handgun stand-in for the service pistol, not a claimed exact
recording of that model. The raw mid-distance file is preserved for editing.

All previous pistol, hand and gunfire assets remain intact. Legacy M1911 stays
playable via F3. Forest lighting, vegetation, ambience and footsteps are unchanged.
Exact source and output hashes, download URLs and geometry/pose reports accompany
unreal/AshgateForest/SourceArt/Viewmodel. No creator endorsement is implied.
