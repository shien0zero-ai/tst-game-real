Further project-authored changes:
- The default service pistol is a fictional field-finish adaptation of the
  retained original model, rather than a claim to reproduce a factory variant.
- Separate blued/satin steel, polymer grip, rubber wrap, controls, crown and
  recessed bore material sections. The original wood variant remains retained.
- Fine roughness/normal grain, sparse hairline scuffs, convex-edge abrasion,
  cavity dirt and subtle patch variation through an original analytic shader.
  Original 4K diffuse/normal/ARM pixels are unchanged; numerical samples from ARM
  classify the grip geometry. Procedural detail is footprint-filtered.
- Four UV sets and vertex flags encode barycentrics, physical triangle altitudes
  and convex-edge eligibility. Wear is restricted to a 0.12-0.45mm band rather
  than drawn along every mesh triangle edge.
- Bevelled 0.85mm rubber grip wrap, 48-sided exposed barrel profile, chamfered
  crown and recessed bore geometry. These are visual game meshes only.
- Finer, weaker leather normal detail and adjusted glove reflectance; existing
  hand anatomy, grip pose and woven sleeves are retained.
- Slightly farther hip/ADS framing and first-person self/contact shadow flags.
  Forest lighting, terrain, foliage and all sound sources remain unchanged.

Preparation: Tools/sample_field_masks.ps1, then Tools/prepare_fieldfinish.py.
Import: full UE Editor -ExecutePythonScript=Tools/fieldfinish_entry.py.
Editable shader: SourceArt/FieldFinish/field_surface.ush (embedded into materials
at import; no engine shader or rendering configuration files are modified).
Source/output hashes and geometry counts: SourceArt/FieldFinish/manifest.json
and geometry-report.json. All prior source and native assets are retained.
