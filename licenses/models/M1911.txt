The source asset was obtained from historical revision
964c79785962160412b9280b1b41b988a122145f, retaining its maker markings and PBR
texture fields. The later README linked above supplies the individual asset
credits and license statement. The intermediate project adapted the gun for an
FPS rig, rescaled it and encoded textures as WebP.

Changes for this project:
- Removed the arms, arm materials, rig, skins and animations; retained only the gun.
- Expanded quantized mesh data into standard glTF data.
- Converted the six embedded gun textures from WebP to PNG without resampling
  or artistic pixel changes.
- Preserved the gun's UVs, normals, texture content and PBR material properties.
- Scaled the 2x source by 0.5 and repositioned it for the Godot viewmodel.
- Exported separate frame (including grip), slide and magazine GLBs and shared
  identical texture files between them.

The model and textures are third-party artwork, not original project-authored
assets. This credit notice applies to the included gun source and its adapted
runtime models and textures. No endorsement by the original author is implied.
