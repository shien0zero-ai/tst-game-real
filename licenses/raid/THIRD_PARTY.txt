The included clips are aim_idle, aim_walk, aim_run, idle, walk, run, death,
hit and reload. None of the intermediate source's 100STYLE / mocap_* clips,
Fab FPS template clips, or fallback BAMEN body mesh are included.

Changes for this project: normalized metre-based bind skeleton and mesh scale;
corrected pouch helper scales; standardized bone names; removed a floating
headset; bound eyes to the head; retargeted the listed Mixamo-convention clips;
added a baked two-bone support-arm solve to the three aim clips for a pistol grip;
resized albedo maps to 512px and normal/packed maps to 256px; renormalized
normal vectors; embedded PNG PBR textures. Body bind height is 1.72 metres.
The original asset creators do not endorse this project.
